# Upcoming ICs

## What it does
- Adds an "Upcoming ICs" page to the left menu.
- Lists every active deal that has an IC meeting still to come (today or later, no decision yet).
- Each row shows the meeting date, the deal, its stage, the deal lead and the facility size.
- Soonest meeting first. Each deal name links to the deal.

## Out of scope
- Passed, lost, on-hold and closed deals.
- Past IC meetings.
- Adding notes, changing dates or booking meetings. It only reads data.

## Open questions
- None. A deal with both a screening and a final meeting coming up shows its soonest one.
